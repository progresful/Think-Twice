Think Twice film storyboard

60 seconds, built on the seven deck slides. Every phone frame is a real screenshot of the running app in the iPhone simulator. The phone stays in the centre and the camera pushes in and scrolls around it; these frames are animated here. Dashed frames are transitions. The line at the bottom of each frame is the on-screen caption.

ShotTransitionCamera zooms into the phone (animated)
The level screens (1b, 4f) and the hold-game lines (4d) were captured with screenshot switches (-skips N, -nag N) that start the app in that state. Reaching "Financially Free" takes 8 skips, so 1b is a time-lapse and carries the caption "over time". Frame 4a is a low-resolution crop of the photo you pasted; the film needs the original file. Bank, card and decline are simulated; one synthetic customer from S-Bank's challenge dataset.
