Why this matters

Almost one in three Europeans cannot cover a single unexpected bill. It is rarely the rent. It is ten small payments nobody stopped.

How we solve it

A coach inside your bank's app that answers from your own transactions, and a lock that makes you pause before the payment that leaks.

One customer in S-Bank's synthetic challenge dataset. 238,84 € times twelve.
How it works

Ask. Understand. Act.

  1. Ask your own money anything."How much did I spend on taxis last month?" Every figure is computed by code from your transactions; the AI only puts it into words.
  2. See three baskets and one number.Fixed costs, irregular costs, everyday spending, and what is safe to spend this week.
  3. Lock what leaks.Past your own limit the payment locks. Earn it back with a short game, or skip and keep the money.
Built for S-mobiili
3,5million

S-Bank customers, one app away.

S-mobiili is used weekly by one in three Finnish adults. Think Twice is designed as a feature inside it: it runs on the structure of the bank's own transaction data, and no Finnish bank yet lets customers talk with their own money.

Figures from S-Bank's challenge brief, Junction 2026. Think Twice is a hackathon prototype and is not part of S-mobiili.

What is real here. The app on this page is a working copy of the iPhone prototype: the figures, the lock, the games and the coach's answers are computed in your browser from one synthetic customer. The bank connection, the card and the declined payment are simulated, and no money moves. The live AI model is used only in the iPhone build. Nothing has been tested with users yet.
